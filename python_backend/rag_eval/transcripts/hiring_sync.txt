Hiring sync - Q4 planning

Maya will own drafting the 30-60-90 template and circulating it for feedback by Wednesday. There was some discussion about raising the referral bonus, but no decision was made -- the group agreed to revisit that after Q4 budget is finalized. Everyone felt good about the pipeline for the backend roles, though sourcing for the design freeze was mentioned as a minor disappointment.
